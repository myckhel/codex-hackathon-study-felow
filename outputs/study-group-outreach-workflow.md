# StudyFellow study-group outreach workflow

Use this conversation workflow whenever the project owner pastes a reply from someone connected to a study group. It turns that reply into a tailored invitation and a tracker entry.

## Context to load

Read `PROJECT_MEMORY.md`, `PROJECT_BRIEF.md`, this file, `study-group-outreach-template.md`, `survey-setup.md`, and `study-groups-tracker.md`. Use `survey-posts.md` for existing tone and the canonical form details.

## From contact reply to draft

Capture only what the contact actually provided:

- Contact name; contact handle only if useful and provided.
- Group name/link, subject/course, learner level, country/context, platform, approximate size.
- Difficult concept or recurring challenge, if mentioned.
- Posting rules and preferred way to request permission.
- Whether the contact is willing to review/share, and their preferred handoff.

Then prepare:

1. A short factual recap, marking missing details as unknown.
2. A ready-to-review post that names the subject/learner context, explains StudyFellow's hackathon research purpose, says participation is voluntary and informal, and fits the group's rules.
3. Check the group's rules and the contact's willingness before preparing outreach. If the contact says survey invitations are not allowed, record the restriction and mark the lead on hold. Do not create or publish a respondent form, prepare a group invitation, or route around the restriction through another member. Revisit only if the rules or permission change.
4. When survey outreach is allowed, create a separate, group-specific survey. Tailor it to actual learner level, subject, and stated friction. When no concept is known yet, ask learners to name one and explain what gets confusing; do not invent the concept or a quiz. Once a concept is identified, prepare a short application/transfer question as a follow-up. Treat technical questions and answer keys as drafts until reviewed by a subject-knowledgeable person.
5. Create a real form and response spreadsheet using `study-group-survey.gs`. Keep email collection off, questions optional, participation voluntary, and avoid collecting names or sensitive details. Verify the respondent page and response destination. Ask the owner immediately before publishing the form to anyone with the link. Record the actual respondent URL after creation. The general link `https://forms.gle/c9ssjT3W9q5GnyyLA` remains available for general recruitment, but must not replace a group-specific link in a lead's tracker row.
6. Prepare a concise, forwardable invitation only when group rules allow surveys and the contact is willing to review/share. Do not post it on the contact's behalf unless the owner separately instructs you to publish to a specific destination.

## Tracker update

The tracker is [StudyFellow | Study Groups Tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Bh-7Xe5SdQJom5SMJ64hwv9-OMUzpO1v2QFZuc6NnMU/edit?gid=0#gid=0). Its header row is ready; it is private to the project owner.

Before adding a row, look for a match by group name/link plus platform and update that row rather than duplicating it. Otherwise append one row with ID `SG-YYYYMMDD-short-slug` and the supplied details, unique survey link, tailored draft, status `Draft — awaiting contact review`, and next action. If form creation is awaiting the owner's Google authorization, leave the survey link blank and status `Survey creation pending authorization`; do not insert the shared general link as a substitute. If publication is pending, record status `Form created — awaiting publish confirmation` and do not say the survey is ready to share. Keep date shared and response count blank until verified. Record only useful contact details; do not add sensitive personal information.

Report the post draft, survey URL, tracker row/status, and any unknowns. A contact's willingness is not permission to publish. Do not send or post unless the owner separately asks for that specific post and destination. After responses identify a concept, summarize the signal as convenience-sample feedback (not a claim about all learners), then prepare the concept-specific practice item and transfer question.
