# Study group outreach template

Hi [Name], hope your day’s going well. I’m doing a small discovery exercise for **StudyFellow**, a hackathon project about helping people learn difficult concepts.

Are you currently part of, or connected to, a study or learning group? If so, would you be comfortable sharing a short survey invitation with the group, provided it fits the group’s rules? I can draft the post for you to review first; you’d only need to share it if you’re happy with it.

If you might be able to help, could you answer these quick questions? Brief answers are fine:

1. What subject or course does the group focus on?
2. What level are the learners at (for example, secondary school, university year, or professional study)?
3. What platform is the group on, and roughly how many members are there?
4. Do you know of a topic or concept members often find difficult? If so, which one?
5. Do the group’s rules allow research or survey invitations? Is there a preferred way to ask?
6. Would you be willing to review and post a tailored invitation, or would you prefer that I prepare text for you to forward?

I’ll use your answers to prepare a brief, relevant post with the survey link. The survey is voluntary and informal, and I’ll treat the responses as feedback from those who choose to participate.
