## Invitation for Rock to review/share

Hi everyone! I’m doing a small discovery exercise for StudyFellow, a hackathon project about helping people learn difficult concepts. I’d value input from master’s students studying cyber security management: which concept or task is hardest to understand or apply, and what specifically gets confusing?

Please share your experience in this short, voluntary survey (about 3 minutes): https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfnx9N0LoQTBD0aSR-Ae5itf8FJaTR39NFZtEM3JuEc03PjIA/viewform

This is informal research, not a graded quiz, and the form does not collect names or email addresses. Please use general learning examples and avoid confidential or security-sensitive details. Your answers will help us choose a topic for an optional short practice exercise. Thank you!

## Survey questions

All six questions are optional. The survey allows a learner to report no current difficulty.

1. Which cyber security management concept or task would you most like help understanding or applying? Name one topic or write None.
2. What specifically is difficult or confusing about that topic?
3. Can you describe a recent study question or practice task where this difficulty appeared? Use a general or fictional example.
4. What did you try to understand it, and how well did that help?
5. What would make a short explanation and practice exercise useful and accessible to you?
6. Would you be willing to try one short, ungraded practice question on a topic identified by this group? Yes / Maybe / No.

After responses arrive, select a learner-identified concept and prepare a reviewed practice question and fresh transfer question. Treat replies as feedback from this convenience sample; do not generalize to all master's students.
